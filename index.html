Questions

Direct answers

Is this an offer of securities?+

No. This site is informational only. Nothing on it constitutes an offer to sell or a solicitation of an offer to buy any security. Any opportunity is presented only to qualified investors, through definitive offering documents.

Who can request the investor presentation?+

Accredited or otherwise qualified investors acting at their own initiative. The request form asks you to confirm this before it can be sent.

What returns should I expect?+

We do not promise returns. Each underwriting memo states target project-level figures for that specific deal, together with the risks we see. Where historical figures appear, past performance does not predict future results.

How is a co-investment structured?+

Deal by deal, through definitive documents prepared by counsel. Structure, economics and reporting are set out in writing before any commitment.

Which markets does Collective Horizon cover?+

High-yield American residential markets, selected for supply, demand and exit depth. The current market list is detailed in the investor presentation.

Do you work with funds and institutions?+

Yes, as counterparties and as partners. Assets are underwritten to institutional buy-box criteria from day one; portfolio pipeline and programmatic structures are discussed directly with the firm.
