The project

Simple to say, demanding to do.

The margin is made at purchase. We only buy when the numbers hold on paper, before the first hammer. Then two roads: sell the renovated house, or keep it and rent it.

1

Buy below value

Off market houses, estates, tired landlords. Every file is priced with an 18 line cost sheet and comparable sales, never with enthusiasm.

2

Renovate on budget

Local licensed contractors, a written scope of works, weekly site checks and photos. The budget is a contract, not a hope.

3

Sell, or rent

Flip files target a sale within months. Rental files are kept for income. Each project states its exit in writing before a dollar moves.

FAQ

Direct questions, direct answers.

Who can take part?+

Accredited or otherwise qualified investors, acting at their own initiative. Qualification is verified before any file goes further.

What is the minimum ticket?+

Tickets start at $10,000. The minimum is set file by file, with the size of the project.

Where does my money sit?+

In the dedicated company of the project, on its own bank account. One house per company, never mixed.

What do I sign?+

Definitive documents prepared with counsel: the company agreement, a subscription document and an investor questionnaire. The website is information, the documents are the deal.

What are the risks?+

Real estate moves, works can overrun, timelines can slip, and a project can lose money. All investments involve risk, including loss of capital. Each file names its risks in writing.

When are distributions made?+

For a flip, at the sale, following the written order of the file: the lender, then costs, then capital, then the split. For a rental, on the schedule of the file. Timing is a target, not a promise.

Can I follow the works?+

Yes. Monthly reporting with photos and spending against budget, and the site can be visited by appointment.
