Privacy policy

Last updated: July 2026.

Who we are

Collective Horizon is a private family office based in Miami, Florida. This site is informational only. It does not offer securities and it does not provide investment, legal or tax advice.

What we collect

The only personal data collected through this site is what you choose to submit through the investor introduction form: your name, your email address, an optional phone number, and an indicative ticket range. We do not collect financial account details, identification documents or any sensitive data through this site.

How the form works

Form submissions are delivered to us by email through FormSubmit, a form delivery service, which processes the submitted fields for the sole purpose of transmitting your message. If that delivery fails, your own email application is opened instead and nothing is transmitted by the site itself.

How we use your data

We use the information you submit for one purpose: responding to your request and, where appropriate, continuing the conversation with you as a prospective co-investor. We do not sell personal data. We do not share it with third parties for marketing.

Cookies and local storage

This site sets no advertising or analytics cookies. It stores two preferences in your browser's local storage: your language choice and your ambient sound choice. They stay on your device and are not transmitted to us.

Retention and your rights

We keep introduction requests for as long as the conversation remains active, then archive or delete them. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data at any time by writing to davidhazout@gmail.com.
