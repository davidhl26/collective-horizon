The principle

Your company. Our method.

The Program is for people who want a real business in the United States without already having a network there. You are the owner and you decide. We bring what takes years to build alone: the houses, the numbers, the contractors, the method.

A real company that you run may, depending on your situation and nationality, support an E-2 treaty investor visa application, which covers the spouse (with work authorization) and children under 21. Eligibility is assessed and the file prepared exclusively by an independent immigration firm that you engage. No visa is ever promised: the decision belongs to the US authorities.

Direct questions

What you should know

Is the visa included in the Program?+

No, and be wary of anyone who sells you one. The Program builds a real company that you run. If your situation allows it, that company can support an E-2 application, prepared by an independent immigration firm you engage directly. We never provide legal advice and no outcome is promised.

How much do I need to invest?+

Your capital stays in your company: expect 100,000 to 200,000 dollars depending on ambition, deployed into your own projects. Added to that are the Program entry fee and your advisers' legal fees, detailed during the assessment.

What income can I expect?+

Your company's, which depend on the market, the projects and your decisions. Every project starts from an underwriting memo with its target figures and its risks in writing. We promise no result, to anyone, ever.

Who does the daily work?+

You run it: purchases, budgets and decisions are yours. We underwrite and execute alongside you: sourcing, analysis, coordination of licensed contractors, resale. It is your company, with a proven method around it.

And my family?+

E-2 status, when granted, covers the spouse and children under 21; the spouse may apply for work authorization. These are public rules of US law, which your immigration attorney will confirm for your specific case.
